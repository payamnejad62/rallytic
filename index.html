15+ Years of Excellence

Our History

Founded in 2009 by former national champion David Carter, Ace Tennis Academy was built on a simple belief: every player deserves world-class coaching, regardless of their starting level.

What began as a small training program on two courts has grown into one of the region's most respected tennis institutions — home to state champions, nationally ranked juniors, and hundreds of recreational players who simply love the sport.

Over the years we've invested in top-tier facilities, expanded our coaching staff, and developed a structured curriculum that takes players from their very first rally to competitive tournament play.
